Skyzen V25 — Responsive Multitrack Preview & Inspector Repair
-------------------------------------------------------------

FIXED
- Created the missing inspector selection labels that could abort clip-property
  loading before any controls became usable.
- Added per-clip audio volume and mute, wired through the timeline, fingerprint,
  render snapshot, and FFmpeg audio mix.
- Trim L / Trim R now trim to the playhead instead of removing one frame per click.
- Cancel obsolete project preview renders when a new edit changes the signature.

IMPROVED
- Continue playing the last valid composite while an updated composite renders.
- Refresh the paused frame at a bounded cadence while dragging inspector sliders.
- Skip neutral grading/rotation filters to reduce FFmpeg work for unadjusted clips.
- Compact panel margins and improve splitter stretch behavior.
- Versioned the preview cache namespace to prevent reuse of older filter graphs.

NOT YET IMPLEMENTED
- Crop, mask shapes, chroma key, blend modes, reverse, speed retiming, HSL/curves,
  and AI tools remain future work; the UI does not claim these controls are active.
