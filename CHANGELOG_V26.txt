Skyzen V26
----------
- Fixed mouse-wheel spinbox behavior: numeric values only change when the
  pointer is over the focused number field.
- Increased adjustment slider resolution by 10x and numeric precision to 3
  decimals.
- Implemented sampled-color chroma key in the FFmpeg preview/export compositor.
- Added key enable, screen eyedropper, color tolerance, edge softness, and
  foreground opacity controls.
- Added chroma settings to edit fingerprints and preview cache signatures.
- Increased preview encode quality to CRF 27 and versioned the preview cache.
- Reduced inspector spacing and improved initial workspace/timeline proportions.
