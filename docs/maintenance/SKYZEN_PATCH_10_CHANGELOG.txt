SKYZEN V43 — PATCH 10 CHANGELOG
===============================

NEW
---
- Real Speed Inspector tab: 0.1x–4x constant playback rate.
- Editable speed curve graph with draggable points and Reset to 1x.
- Speed curve source-time integration and FFmpeg inverse timestamp mapping.
- Live playback rate integration; retimed/keyframed clips no longer bypass the timeline decoder path.
- Audio atempo processing for constant speed; average speed for speed-curve audio.
- Frame-snapped keyframes for color-grading properties.
- Keyframe Curve timeline toolbar action with property selection, editable graph points and easing presets.
- Keyframe easing modes: Linear, Ease in, Ease out, Ease in-out, Hold.
- FFmpeg frame-evaluated animated grade expressions and keyframe-aware scrub preview.

- Increased visibility icon size and changed its glyph to a larger eye-like mark.
- Reduced timeline layer-header width by 18 px (156 -> 138 px).
- Removed hover tooltips from clip/layer controls to keep the timeline unobstructed.
- Updated regression coverage for new speed/keyframe behavior.
